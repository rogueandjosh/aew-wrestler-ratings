What is Best Match of the Week?

Each week, every match from that week's shows is compared by its Cagematch.net community rating — the highest-rated match becomes that week's Best Match of the Week.

You'll also see this indicated with a gold * next to the relevant wrestler(s) or team(s) on the main rankings pages — click it for a quick popup with the match details. And on the All Wrestler Statistics view, the ⭐ column shows a running total of how many times each wrestler or team has featured in a Best Match, cumulative up to whichever week you have selected.
