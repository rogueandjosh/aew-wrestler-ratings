13 Oct 2026 DATA 🔄 Ratings Refresh

A few behind-the-scenes tweaks have been made to how some matches are counted, and every week back to June 2019 has been refreshed to match.

  • Tag teams within trios — once a tag team has had 5 trios matches in a row without a tag match of their own, any further trios matches no longer count towards them as a tag team. For example, Brodido's recent matches alongside Hangman Page now count for the trio, but not for Brodido as a tag team
  • Championship ratings — tag team and trios title ratings now sit in line with the singles titles
  • Recently Dropped Out — now lists only those who dropped out that week
  • Week dates — the date ranges shown for some archive weeks now match the shows in that week
  • Plus a few small data corrections, such as tidier stipulation names

The result is some small rating changes here and there over the years. Everything — rankings, the archive, wrestler pages and championships — is updated and ready to explore.

2 Oct 2026 FEATURE 🎯 Match Filters on Wrestler Pages

Every wrestler and team stats page now has a Match Filters panel, just below the rating graph, for digging into their matches.

  • Format — singles, tag team, trios, bigger tag matches, multi-way matches, and battle royals & gauntlets
  • Stakes — title matches, contender & eliminator matches, and tournaments like the Continental Classic and Owen Hart Cup
  • Stipulation — every stipulation they've had, with similar variants grouped together (e.g. every city's Street Fight), plus quick options for "Any stipulation" or "No stipulation"
  • Show Type — Pay Per View, Weekly TV, TV Specials and more. Once you pick one, a Show filter appears so you can narrow it down further: Dynamite, Collision or Rampage, or individual PPVs like Revolution and Full Gear, in calendar order (All In editions are grouped together under one All In)
  • Card Position — Main Event, Opener or Other

Tick as many options as you like in each filter. The numbers next to each option update as you go, so you can see how many matches fit with your other choices, and a summary shows the match count, average rating and best match for your selection.

The Match Quality Distribution, Recent Match Ratings and Best & Worst Performances sections all update with the filters, and they work alongside the year buttons. The rating graph and rating stats above stay as they are, since weekly ratings cover all of a wrestler's matches.

Filtered views are saved in the page link, so you can share exactly what you're looking at.

3 Sep 2026 FEATURE ⭐ Best Match of the Week

Every week now has its own Best Match of the Week — automatically identified as whichever match earned the highest Cagematch community rating that week.

  • A gold * appears next to any wrestler or team who competed in that week's top match, across every division tab and the Select Multiple views — click it for a popup with full match details, click again to close
  • Teams get credit for competing as themselves, or for having every member involved in a bigger unit's win (e.g. a tag team gets credit if both members were part of a winning Trios match)
  • A ⭐ column on the All Wrestler Statistics page shows a running total of Best Match appearances per wrestler/team, cumulative up to whichever week is selected

Example — clicking the asterisk next to Will Ospreay's name after a big Aug '26 Week 5 title match shows:

⭐ Best Match of the Week
Date: Aug 30, 2026
Event: All In London '26
Rating: 9.64
Match Type: One on One
Stakes: Title Match
Wrestlers: Will Ospreay, Kenny Omega

Full historical backfill complete — every week from June 2019 through today has Best Match data.

3 Jun 2026 FEATURE 🤝 Faction Display Names

Tag and trios team names now reflect real-world factions where applicable. Instead of listing individual names, combinations of faction members display under their faction name — so Castagnoli, Moxley & Yuta becomes Death Riders, for example.

How it works:

  • Any 2+ members of a faction competing together show the faction name
  • Partial combos (a faction member teaming with an outsider) display as the faction name alongside the non-member's name
  • Overrides preserved — teams with their own established identity (e.g. ProtoShita) keep their name even when members belong to a faction
  • Historically accurate — faction membership is applied week-by-week, so archive weeks reflect the correct groupings for that period

Faction names appear across the main rankings, the statistics tab, recently dropped out sections, and individual wrestler pages.

30 May 2026 NEW 🏆 Championships Page Launched

A brand-new Championships page is now live, tracking the prestige of every AEW title through the same match-quality rating system used for the wrestler rankings.

Rather than tracking wins and losses, title prestige is measured by the quality of championship defenses — a title defended in a string of highly-rated matches climbs the rankings; one with forgettable defenses drifts back toward the baseline.

What's on the page:

  • All active AEW championships ranked by current rating
  • Belt icons colour-coded by title (gold for AEW titles, silver for non-AEW)
  • Sortable columns — rank, rating, streak, change, and more
  • Toggle between Current Reign and All-Time stats for each title
  • Peak and floor ratings for both the current reign and historical all-time records
  • Best and worst individual match ratings per reign
  • Week archive navigation — browse any week back to June 2019
  • "Recently Dropped Out" section for titles that have gone inactive
  • Mobile-friendly horizontal scroll
